- Intercom inbox UI, viewed from the screenshot on https://zapier.com/blog/best-customer-support-apps/: separate queue, thread, and customer context; adapt message-to-state causality and sparse column hierarchy. Do not copy the logo, colors, icons, exact panel dimensions, or conversation text.

Adapt those hierarchy principles; no brand assets or copied product skin. Motion grammar is independently authored rather than claimed to reproduce a reference video.

## Concept-specific choreography

1. Instant support — Indigo Cloud. Camera enters the selected thread; a typing indicator becomes an answer; an order progress strip fills; the same thread resolves. Close thread → order detail → system reveal.
2. Sales qualification — Warm Neutral. A customer request yields two intent chips. The resulting lead card physically travels New → Qualified → Meeting. Camera hands from the chat to the lead's destination.
3. Internal knowledge — Midnight Violet. A query pulls handbook and policy documents toward a private search surface. Sources become citation chips and a grounded answer. Search closeup → response inspection → sources reveal.
4. Unified channels — Electric Teal. Web, email and mobile messages travel into corresponding rows of one inbox. Staggered channel paths end in individually resolved rows. Device cluster → inbox arrival → complete inbox.
5. Human handoff — Indigo Cloud. A request creates a persistent context summary; its movement triggers a route to an available specialist. Assignment and join state complete the same conversation.
6. Conversation analytics — Midnight Violet. UI-bound counters populate, a resolution line draws across its chart, and channel distribution grows. Metric detail → trend tracking → complete dashboard. Values are illustrative demo data, not real product claims.
7. Team access — Warm Neutral with olive accent. Selecting a member opens a permission drawer; role and toggles update, save confirms, drawer releases back to the same table.
8. Multilingual support — Orchid Cloud. A Spanish question passes through a translation control into an English support workspace; the reply returns in Spanish. Camera follows each direction.
9. Automated resolution — Electric Teal. Message → intent → eligibility check → processed refund. A fallback specialist route remains visibly secondary. Camera follows execution across the persistent workflow.
10. Conversation recap — Warm Neutral. Transcript scroll exposes the decision; the summarize control moves toward an extracted structured recap and assigned onboarding task. Same transcript remains visible in the result.

UI-safe area: final compositions inside 153–1767 horizontal pixels of the 1920-wide authoring stage. Camera excursions deliberately crop chrome, never primary content. Fixed viewport scaling is separate from authored camera motion. Time is owned by the GSAP master timeline and OPENER.seek(t); fonts are awaited before capture.
