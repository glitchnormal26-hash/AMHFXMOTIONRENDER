# PROMPT PACK — Foto Cinematic dari Text Prompt
Semua prompt di bawah ini dirancang untuk model text-to-image fotorealistis (ChatGPT image / GPT-Image / Midjourney / Flux). Pola tetap: **subjek kecil • kamera tinggi • satu sumber cahaya dominan • ruang negatif luas • grade hangat-dingin**.

---

```

Composition is strongly cinematic and asymmetrical, with the pedestrian positioned
slightly left of center and surrounded by expansive negative space. The camera is very
high above street level, approximately 45-60 degree downward angle, creating a graphic

Natural atmospheric depth, soft misty sunlight, realistic wet-surface reflections, subtle
bloom, restrained contrast, slightly desaturated urban colors, warm highlights with cool
neutral shadows, fine film grain, cinematic color grading, photorealistic texture,

No visible facial emphasis, no logos, no readable advertisements, no excessive
pedestrians, no clutter.

```

## 4) NEGATIVE / PENJAGA REALISME
Selalu sertakan (atau pakai negative prompt): `no logos, no readable advertisements, no readable text, no watermark, no extra people, no clutter, no oversaturated neon, no flat lighting, no centered subject, no close-up faces`.

## 5) CHECKLIST QC HASIL GENERATE
1. Subjek ≤ ±8% luas frame? 2. Bayangan panjang satu arah? 3. Ada refleksi/permukaan basah atau haze? 4. Garis lantai membentuk pola geometris? 5. Grain halus & grade hangat-dingin? 6. Bebas teks/logo bocor?
