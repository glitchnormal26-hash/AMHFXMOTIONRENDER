# PROMPT PACK — Cara Membuat Poster Cybergoth Y2K Style
## Art direction: Liquid Chrome Romance (Gothic Y2K Cyber-Glam)

## MASTER PROMPT (ditampilkan bertahap di BAB 05)

Create an original premium Y2K cyber-glam editorial poster featuring a photorealistic beauty portrait, dark romantic gothic atmosphere, sculptural liquid chrome, and silver-lavender color grading.

Use oversized futuristic editorial typography with polished silver bevels, realistic metallic reflections, elongated gothic-inspired letterforms, and carefully controlled 3D depth.

Build a layered composition with a dominant portrait, small editorial detail panels, abstract chrome ornaments, sculptural metallic hearts, reflective chains, and subtle futuristic interface graphics.

Use soft beauty lighting on the face, cool rim lighting along the hair, deep obsidian shadows, precise silver highlights, and restrained lavender atmospheric glow.

Maintain realistic skin texture, natural facial anatomy, elegant negative space, and clear visual hierarchy.

Original fictional subject, original typography, original ornament design, no copied logos or existing poster layouts.

Vertical editorial poster, 3:4 aspect ratio.

### Enam komponen prompt (kerangka pengajaran)
1. SUBJECT — fokus visual
2. ART DIRECTION — dunia visual (dark romantic cyber-glam, futuristic fashion editorial)
3. MATERIAL — reflective silver, polished bevels, liquid-metal curves, realistic environmental reflections
4. LIGHTING — soft beauty light wajah, cool rim lighting rambut, highlight tegas material metalik
5. COMPOSITION — posisi portrait, ukuran headline, inset, foreground, negative space
6. QUALITY CONTROL — wajah realistis, material meyakinkan, ornamen tidak mengambil alih
