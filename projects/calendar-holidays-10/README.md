# Calendar Holiday Motion — 10 concepts

Temporary production branch for ten individual 10-second calendar-date motion graphics.

Render target:
- 3840×2160 (4K UHD)
- 30 fps
- H.264 MP4
- CRF 18 / preset medium (medium-bitrate VBR quality target)
- no audio
- no branding

Concepts:
1. Midnight Flip — New Year — 01 JAN
2. Ribbon Heart — Valentine's Day — 14 FEB
3. Eco Orbit — Earth Day — 22 APR
4. Kinetic Grid Shift — Labour Day — 01 MAY
5. Glass Terrarium — Environment Day — 05 JUN
6. Ribbon Flag Sweep — Indonesia Independence Day — 17 AUG
7. Pattern Bloom — Batik Day — 02 OCT
8. Light Monument — Heroes' Day — 10 NOV
9. Floral Cut Paper — Mother's Day — 22 DEC
10. Winter Fold — Christmas Day — 25 DEC

All scenes use deterministic GSAP timelines exposed through `window.OPENER`.
