# AI Chatbot Dashboard Promo — Creative Plan

Project: RelayAI AI Chatbot Dashboard Promo  
Branch: `render/ai-chatbot-dashboard-promo`  
Scene: `project/ai-chatbot-dashboard/scene.html`  
Profile: SaaS Motion Graphic Explainer / Electric Teal palette  
Duration: 12s, 1920x1080

## Visual argument

One persistent customer conversation becomes the continuity carrier for the whole product story. The AI reply causes a glowing route to move through the same dashboard world, revealing analytics, team routing, and multi-channel support without resetting the scene into independent slides.

## Beat map

1. **Hook — 0.0–1.45s**  
   Dashboard shell lands while the camera pushes in. Fragment headline: “Every chat becomes action.”

2. **Conversation — 1.45–4.15s**  
   Customer message → AI thinking → typed AI response → suggested action. The conversation remains the primary hero.

3. **Analytics — 4.15–6.75s**  
   The AI action emits the persistent route line. Camera trucks toward analytics while counters, chart, and channel bars resolve in the same shell.

4. **User routing — 6.75–9.1s**  
   Camera catches the next destination. User rows and smart assignment queues appear as the route’s operational result.

5. **Multi-channel — 9.1–10.35s**  
   A connected-channel cluster grows from the same workspace language, showing web chat, email, messaging, and API.

6. **Payoff — 10.35–12.0s**  
   Camera pulls out to restore the whole product world. Closing fragment: “One AI. Every conversation.”

## Continuity carrier

The glowing route line originates from the AI reply and persists through analytics, routing, and multi-channel states. Camera velocity, shell identity, palette, panel material, and channel language stay continuous throughout.

## Camera states

- Hook: push-in / focus.
- Conversation: tighter reframe for readability.
- Analytics: rightward truck with faster settle.
- User routing: catch-up / opposite-side reframe.
- Multi-channel: controlled pull-back.
- Payoff: wider final lock.
